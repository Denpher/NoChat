Audio library previews (track-1.mp3 … track-8.mp3) are short excerpts of
"SoundHelix Song 1–8" by T. Schürger, https://www.soundhelix.com — free to use.
They are bundled inside the app so previews never break because of a third-party link.
Replace any file with your own MP3 of the same name to change a track.
