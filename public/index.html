Privacy Policy

Privacy is our first policy.

At NoChat, your privacy is protected by design and code, not just legal promises.

1. No Third-Party Trackers

We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any third-party tracking cookies. Your browsing activity never leaves the application.

2. Strict Email & Identity Confidentiality

Your email address is strictly confidential and is only visible to you. It is never exposed in user profiles, feeds, or search APIs.

3. Direct Content Control

You can edit your messages within 15 minutes and delete your messages or reels at any time. When deleted, your media is permanently wiped from storage.

4. Security by Default

All traffic is encrypted via TLS with Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS max-age=31536000 preload), secure HttpOnly cookies, and strict CSP headers.
